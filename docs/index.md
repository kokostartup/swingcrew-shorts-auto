# SwingCrew Shorts Auto

**Publishing automation tool for golf creators.** Schedule and post short-form golf instructional videos directly to your TikTok creator account.

## Who is this for?

Golf coaches, instructors, and short-form content creators who want to streamline publishing their pre-produced short videos to TikTok without manual upload.

## How it works

1. **Connect your TikTok account** — Authorize via TikTok's official OAuth 2.0 flow. Each creator authorizes their own account independently.
2. **Upload pre-rendered videos** — The tool reads MP4 files from your local storage and uploads them to your TikTok account.
3. **Choose flow** — Send to your TikTok inbox for manual review, or direct post with caption.
4. **Track status** — The tool polls TikTok's API until publishing completes.

## Features

- OAuth 2.0 secure authentication
- Multiple creator accounts supported independently
- Inbox (draft) and direct post modes
- Caption and post settings customization
- Automatic token refresh
